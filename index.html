01 / About

My work bridges atmospheric science and management: chemical transport and numerical weather modelling, aerosol–meteorology interactions, and reduced-complexity models that translate emissions into policy-relevant, multi-scale airsheds across India.

National airshed management framework

Built a national-scale framework under the PAVITRA project, applying the reduced-complexity model InMAP-PAVITRA to delineate policy-relevant airsheds across India.

02

Multi-scale modelling integration

Integrated urban → regional → national modelling approaches to generate coherent, policy-relevant insights for air quality management in India.

Developed a chemical transport framework quantifying the air quality and climate impacts of aerosols transported from the Indo-Gangetic Plains to Northeast India.

03 / Model Development

InMAP‑PAVITRA

Collaborative effort

An India‑specific adaptation of InMAP developed collaboratively under the PAVITRA project, built to facilitate multiscale air quality management — from city to airshed to national scale.

Model development is a joint effort across four institutions, pairing atmospheric science expertise with the policy questions Indian agencies need answered. My contribution sits in the model development and its national‑scale application to airshed delineation and source attribution.

InMAP‑Extended

Independent effort

A personally developed extension of InMAP, the reduced‑complexity air quality model — built to make source‑attributed PM2.5 assessment practical at India‑relevant scales, and to push past InMAP's annual‑average ceiling so that sub‑annual policies can be evaluated.

Developed and maintained outside of any funded mandate, on my own initiative. The aim is a model that policy analysts can actually run: fast enough for scenario sweeps, transparent enough to audit, and structured around the airshed and source‑attribution questions Indian air quality management keeps asking.

What it adds

Sub-annual policy evaluation

Goes beyond InMAP's annual-average limit — evaluating shorter-term interventions.

RapidCTM

Independent effort

A rapid chemical transport model built on a physics‑informed neural network, trained to reproduce concentration fields at a fraction of the cost of a conventional chemical transport simulation.

Written on my own initiative, aimed at the gap between reduced‑complexity models and full CTMs.
